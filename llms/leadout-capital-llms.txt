# Leadout Capital

> Leadout Capital is an early-stage venture capital fund based in Portola Valley, California, founded in
> 2018 by Ali Rosenthal. The firm leads, co-leads, or takes a significant position in pre-seed and seed
> rounds, organizing its thesis around "founder-market fit" — founders with a unique market insight born of
> lived experience and demonstrable, empathy-grounded customer expertise. Leadout is a limited-partner
> relationship of Foundry Group's partner-fund network. It has no public API; this profile is a company
> record in the API Evangelist network.

## Focus

- Stage: pre-seed and seed; leads, co-leads, or takes a significant position.
- Thesis: founder-market fit — unique market insight grounded in the founding team's lived experience.
- Preferred characteristics: strong network effects, unique data sets, organic and viral growth, strong unit economics, long-term defensibility.
- Sectors seen in the portfolio: SaaS, Vertical SaaS, Marketplace, Healthcare, Consumer & Commerce, Infra / Developer Tools.
